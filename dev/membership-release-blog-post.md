---

The most awaited feature for Buy Me Coffee is finally here: **content monetization with memberships**.

Buy Me Coffee started as a simple way for creators, bloggers, educators, nonprofits, and independent publishers to accept tips and donations directly through Stripe and PayPal. With version **1.2.6**, it now becomes a stronger monetization tool for WordPress creators who want to earn from premium content without relying on Patreon, Ko-fi, Buy Me a Coffee, WooCommerce, or another hosted platform.

With the new Memberships feature, you can create paid membership levels, restrict posts or pages, show teaser content, display a paywall CTA, and let supporters become recurring members directly from your own WordPress site.

## Why Memberships Matter

Creators need more than one-time donations. A tip jar is useful, but recurring income is what helps creators publish consistently, build a community, and keep their work independent.

That is why this release focuses on monetization. You can now use Buy Me Coffee as a lightweight WordPress membership plugin, paywall plugin, donation plugin, and recurring subscription tool in one place.

This is especially useful for:

- Bloggers who want to publish members-only posts
- Writers who want to monetize premium articles or newsletters
- Educators who want to sell paid lessons or tutorials
- Coaches who want to share private resources
- Podcasters and video creators who want supporter-only updates
- Nonprofits and community groups that need recurring giving
- Independent publishers who want a simple WordPress paywall

Payments still go directly to your own Stripe or PayPal account. Buy Me Coffee does not take a commission from your memberships, donations, tips, or subscriptions.

## Create Paid Membership Levels

The new Memberships screen lets you create multiple membership levels for your audience.

Each level can include:

- Monthly or yearly pricing
- Level name and description
- Reward or benefit bullets
- Active or inactive status
- Checkout preview links
- Content access rules

You can create simple tiers like "Supporter", "Member", and "Pro Member", or use your own names that match your audience and content strategy.

## Protect Premium Posts and Pages

Buy Me Coffee 1.2.6 adds content access controls directly inside the WordPress editor.

When editing a post or page, you can mark the content as:

- Free for everyone
- Paid for members only

For paid content, you can choose which membership levels can access the full post. You can also set a preview word count, so non-members can read a short teaser before seeing the paywall.

This makes it easy to monetize:

- Premium blog posts
- Paid tutorials
- Private updates
- Member-only newsletters
- Course-style lessons
- Download pages
- Community announcements

## Built-In WordPress Paywall CTA

When a visitor does not have access to a protected post, Buy Me Coffee shows a clean paywall CTA after the preview content.

The paywall can show:

- Membership level names
- Prices
- Billing intervals
- Descriptions
- Reward bullets
- Join buttons

When a visitor clicks to join, they are sent into the membership checkout flow. After checkout, they can return to the content they were trying to read.

This creates a smoother conversion path for paid content. Visitors see the value of the content first, then get a clear reason to become a member.

## Manage Active Members

The Memberships area now includes an active members dashboard.

From one screen, you can review:

- Member name and email
- Membership level
- Billing interval
- Renewal date
- Subscription status
- Subscription details

You can also jump directly to a member's subscription detail page or cancel a membership when needed.

## Recurring Stripe Subscriptions

Memberships are powered by Buy Me Coffee's existing recurring subscription system.

Supporters can subscribe monthly or yearly through Stripe. Renewals, cancellations, failed payments, and status changes are handled through Stripe webhooks and synced into WordPress.

This gives creators a direct recurring revenue model without needing a heavy ecommerce setup.

## Donations, Tips, and Memberships Together

The best part is that memberships do not replace the existing donation workflow.

You can still use Buy Me Coffee for:

- One-time donations
- Tips
- Stripe payments
- PayPal payments
- Recurring supporter subscriptions
- Supporter wall
- Donation forms
- Shortcodes and Gutenberg block
- Refunds from WordPress
- Activity logs
- Subscriber account page

Now you can combine donations and paid content in the same plugin.

For example, you can add a donation button to free posts, create paid posts for members, and give subscribers a place to manage their account with the `[buymecoffee_account]` shortcode.

## No WooCommerce Required

Many WordPress membership workflows require WooCommerce, paid addons, or a full LMS setup.

Buy Me Coffee is intentionally focused. If you only need paid content, memberships, recurring supporter subscriptions, and direct Stripe or PayPal payments, you can keep the setup much lighter.

That makes it a good fit for creators who want to start monetizing content quickly without building a full ecommerce site.

## A Self-Hosted Patreon Alternative

Hosted creator platforms are convenient, but they also control the platform, checkout experience, customer relationship, and sometimes the fees.

Buy Me Coffee keeps the monetization workflow inside WordPress.

You own your site. You own your audience relationship. Your payment gateway sends funds directly to your own account.

For creators looking for a self-hosted Patreon alternative, Ko-fi alternative, or Buy Me a Coffee alternative for WordPress, this release is a major step forward.

## How to Start Monetizing Content

To use the new membership feature:

1. Update Buy Me Coffee to version 1.2.6.
2. Go to **Buy Me Coffee -> Memberships**.
3. Create one or more membership levels.
4. Open a post or page in the WordPress editor.
5. Set the content access to **Paid**.
6. Choose the membership levels that can access the content.
7. Publish the post and test the paywall as a non-member.

You can also create a subscriber account page using:

```text
[buymecoffee_account]
```

This lets logged-in members view their subscriptions and recent transactions.
